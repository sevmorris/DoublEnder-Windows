DoublEnder
==========

Pick your microphone, press the big button, and your recording is saved
to your Desktop. Send that file to your producer when you're done.

Starting it
  Windows: double-click DoublEnder.exe. Keep this folder together; the
           app needs the files beside it.
  Linux:   double-click DoublEnder, or run ./DoublEnder in a terminal.

Using it
  - The microphone button (left of the meter) picks the input.
  - The meter shows that sound is coming in.
  - PRESS TO RECORD starts; PRESS TO STOP saves.
  - The gear sets the file name, notes, and AAC or WAV.
  - Drag the faceplate to move the window. The small buttons in the
    top-right corner minimise and close it (Ctrl+Q also quits).

If the app or computer crashes mid-recording, open DoublEnder again: it
offers to recover the recording as a WAV file.

Windows: if the meter never moves, open Settings > Privacy & security >
Microphone and turn on "Let desktop apps access your microphone".
